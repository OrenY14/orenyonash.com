How an engagement works

Change is led by the organisation's own people, with the CEO behind it.

  1. 01

    The CEO owns the process

    and is the go-to person for decisions, which is what overcomes resistance to change.

  2. 02

    A champion, appointed by the CEO

    keeps every project team moving, and removes most work from the CEO.

  3. 03

    Interviews first

    Oren interviews the teams, observes the work on the floor where needed, and learns the processes before anything is proposed.

  4. 04

    A one or two day management workshop

    gives leaders the language and logic, and they choose the projects. Every hospital has its own focus areas, for example the ER, the operating rooms, the ICU, the delivery room and more.

  5. 05 · The heart of the method

    A two or three day staff workshop

    in which each project team defines its project, gets trained on the required skills, and starts work. Teams include everyone who touches the process: the head of department, doctors, nurses, the secretary, even cleaning staff.

    This is the heart of the method: the knowledge, the know-how, the buy-in and the ongoing improvement grow out of these teams, because they are gained by doing, not only by talking in interviews.

  6. 06

    Hands-on support for weeks

    working with the teams on implementation.

  7. 07

    Every team sets a clear goal, measures it and reports to the CEO
